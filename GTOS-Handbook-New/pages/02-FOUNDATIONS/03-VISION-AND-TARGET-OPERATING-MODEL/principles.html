Strategic Principles

Strategic Principles


  • Model trade reality before software workflow.
  • Optimize for end-to-end evidence, not local data volume.
  • Automate decisions only after authority and knowledge boundaries are explicit.
  • Treat interoperability as semantic governance, not payload transport.
  • Build for jurisdictional variation without fragmenting core meaning.
  • Preserve human accountability while increasing machine capability.
  • Prefer reversible implementation choices and durable business semantics.
  • Design operations and recovery as part of architecture.
