Domain Implementation Template


Every Domain pack shall include:


  • Purpose, scope, owner, external authorities, and non-goals.
  • Ubiquitous language and subject identities.
  • Context map and upstream/downstream relationships.
  • Aggregates, entities, value objects, invariants, and state machines.
  • Decisions, authority, policies, exceptions, appeals, and corrections.
  • Commands, Events, queries, API, webhook, file, and tool contracts.
  • Durable workflows, deadlines, retries, idempotency, reconciliation, and compensation.
  • Database schema, keys, constraints, indexes, versions, migrations, lineage, and retention.
  • Portal journeys, personas, accessibility, localization, and error states.
  • AI use cases, autonomy, context, tools, evaluation, and prohibited behavior.
  • Security, privacy, threat model, tenant isolation, and audit.
  • SLOs, dashboards, alerts, runbooks, capacity, backup, and recovery.
  • Tests, conformance evidence, rollout, migration, compatibility, and deprecation.
  • Worked scenarios including success, partial failure, contradiction, correction, and dispute.

    • A Domain review is rejected if any section is replaced by “TBD” without an owner, due date, and risk decision.
