Architecture Review Checklist

Architecture Review Checklist


  • Canon dependencies identified
  • Truth ownership explicit
  • Identity and time semantics explicit
  • State and Event semantics valid
  • Observations separated from authoritative facts
  • Decision authority and evidence defined
  • Intent precedes execution
  • Security, privacy, resilience, and operations included
  • Migration and correction behavior defined
  • Conformance evidence available
