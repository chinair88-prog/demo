Conformance Levels


Level 1 — Semantic #


Uses GTOS terminology and preserves foundational distinctions.


Level 2 — Architectural #


Implements ownership, contracts, temporal semantics, authority, security, and evidence.


Level 3 — Operational #


Demonstrates SLOs, recovery, incident evidence, policy enforcement, and continuing validation.


Level 4 — Federated #


Interoperates across organizations while preserving authority and jurisdiction.
