Purpose#

A Decision Record enables explanation, appeal, audit, learning, and outcome monitoring.

Minimum Record#

  • decision identity and type;
  • subject;
  • authority identity and basis;
  • decision time and knowledge cutoff;
  • evidence references;
  • policy and rule versions;
  • recommendations considered;
  • alternatives;
  • uncertainty;
  • decision outcome;
  • explanation;
  • approval or escalation;
  • expected effect;
  • later outcome references;
  • correction or appeal status.

AI-Assisted Decision#

Where AI contributes, the record additionally captures model route, model version, prompt or task version, retrieval set, tool results, calibration context, and safety checks. Private model reasoning is not required. Reproducible evidence and structured rationale are required.

Outcome Learning#

Decision quality SHALL be evaluated against later outcomes without rewriting what was knowable at the time. A wrong prediction, a poor decision, and an unsuccessful execution are distinct failure classes and demand different remediation.
