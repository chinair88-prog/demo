Dimensions#

Jurisdiction may derive from party establishment, facility location, goods origin or destination, transport route, data subject location, regulator authority, contract choice of law, payment system, or data-processing region.

No single country field is sufficient.

Jurisdiction Context#

A jurisdiction context records applicable dimensions, evidence, effective time, uncertainty, and decision authority. It may be computed, but the computation remains traceable.

Policy Application#

Policies declare jurisdiction scope and precedence. When multiple regimes apply, GTOS preserves each obligation and identifies conflict-resolution authority.

Change Over Time#

Jurisdiction can change during a value stream. A shipment route change, ownership transfer, or new regulatory designation may trigger re-evaluation.

Data Architecture#

Data contracts identify residency, transfer, localization, and retention constraints. Derived datasets inherit applicable restrictions unless an approved transformation changes classification.

Operations#

Incident response and support access respect jurisdiction. Emergency access does not erase notification, evidence, or review obligations.

AI#

Agent mandates and model routing are jurisdiction-aware. An Agent approved for one region SHALL not silently process restricted data in another.
