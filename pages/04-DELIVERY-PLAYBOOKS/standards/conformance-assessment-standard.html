Assessment Scope#

An assessment declares organization, product, domains, environments, version, jurisdictions, and exclusions.

Evidence Method#

Assessors evaluate documents, registries, schemas, code, tests, configurations, operational records, incidents, and interviews. Statements without evidence do not establish conformance.

Finding Classes#

  • conforming;
  • observation;
  • minor non-conformance;
  • major non-conformance;
  • critical non-conformance;
  • approved exception.

Critical Findings#

Critical findings include unowned authoritative truth, unauthorized automated execution, erased governed history, material security bypass, inability to reconstruct high-impact decisions, or false claims of regulatory authority.

Remediation#

Each finding has owner, impact, root condition, remediation, compensating control, deadline, and verification. Closure requires evidence.

Certification Boundary#

Conformance applies only to the assessed scope and version. A compliant platform does not make every deployed extension compliant.

Continuous Conformance#

Automated checks monitor schemas, registry completeness, policy tests, dependency status, traceability, and operational controls. Periodic review covers semantic and governance concerns not fully automatable.
