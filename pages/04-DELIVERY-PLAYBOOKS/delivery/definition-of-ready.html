A work item is ready for implementation only when:

  • the business outcome and affected subject are explicit;
  • truth ownership is known;
  • required identity and temporal semantics are defined;
  • state, invariants, Command, Event, and Decision behavior are understood;
  • contracts and acceptance criteria exist;
  • security classification and authority are defined;
  • AI involvement, if any, has an approved output type and mandate;
  • migration and compatibility impact are known;
  • observability and operational expectations are included;
  • open questions have owners and dates.

A backlog item that says only “build API,” “add AI,” or “sync data” is not ready.
