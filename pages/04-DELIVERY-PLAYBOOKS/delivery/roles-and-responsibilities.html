Role Primary Accountability SHALL Approve
Executive Sponsor transformation outcome, funding, organizational authority mandate, scope, major risk
Architecture Council Canon, Constitution, cross-domain coherence normative changes and major exceptions
Chief Architect end-to-end reference architecture architecture baseline and release conformance
Domain Owner authoritative propositions and business outcomes domain charter, invariants, decisions
Product Manager value, roadmap, adoption, service outcomes product scope and measurable acceptance
Domain Architect domain model, contracts, integration aggregates, Commands, Events, policies
Data Owner/Steward data meaning, quality, retention, access data contract and quality rules
AI Owner AI purpose, evaluation, outcome, and safety mandate, evaluation, deployment
Security Architect threat, identity, policy, privacy, incident controls security design and residual risk
Platform Owner shared capability service quality platform contracts and SLOs
Engineering Lead implementation quality and delivery evidence technical design, tests, release artifact
SRE/Operations Owner reliability, support, recovery, incident readiness SLO, runbook, recovery evidence
Partner Integration Lead external participant readiness certification and cutover

Accountability Rule#

Approval is not attendance. An approver confirms that the artifact is correct within their authority and that unresolved risks are explicit. One person MAY hold multiple roles in a small implementation, but conflicting duties and high-impact approvals SHOULD remain independently reviewed.
