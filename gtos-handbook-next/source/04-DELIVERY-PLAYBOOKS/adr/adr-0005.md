# ADR-0005 — Domain Ownership over Shared Database

## Status

Accepted

## Context

GTOS requires durable semantics and auditable architecture decisions across independently evolving technologies and organizations.

## Decision

Domains own propositions; integration uses governed contracts.

## Consequences

- The decision becomes reviewable and testable.
- Implementations must preserve the declared semantic boundary.
- Exceptions require scope, owner, expiry, and review.
- Future replacement technology may vary while the architectural obligation remains.

## Canon Traceability

CANON-001, CANON-002, and additional Canons applicable to the decision.
