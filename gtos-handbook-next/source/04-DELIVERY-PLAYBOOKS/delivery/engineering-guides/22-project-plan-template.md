# GTOS Project Plan Template

## Workstreams

A GTOS project plan normally includes business architecture, domain design, contracts and integration, data and migration, AI and decision intelligence, security and privacy, platform and infrastructure, engineering enablement, partner onboarding, testing, operations, change management, and benefit realization.

## Milestones

Milestones align to delivery stage gates rather than arbitrary percentage completion. Recommended milestones are mandate approval, Reference Alignment, domain ownership approval, contract baseline, operational design approval, first vertical slice, partner certification, migration rehearsal, production readiness, controlled launch, and stabilization exit.

## Dependency Management

Dependencies identify provider, consumer, required artifact, version, due date, acceptance evidence, and fallback. “Platform dependency” is too vague. The plan states whether the dependency is an identity capability, policy contract, Event topic, data product, environment, partner decision, or operational readiness item.

## Critical Path

The critical path often passes through semantic and organizational decisions rather than coding. Unresolved truth ownership, authority, partner mandate, historical migration, or jurisdiction policy can block safe implementation. Project reporting SHALL expose these conditions rather than presenting engineering activity as equivalent progress.

## Status Reporting

Status includes business outcome, stage-gate evidence, decisions required, high risks, architecture debt, contract readiness, migration reconciliation, security findings, SLO readiness, partner status, and benefit measures. Red status is used when a critical semantic or authority decision is absent even if code delivery is on schedule.

## Change Control

Scope changes are assessed for Canon, domain, contract, data, AI, security, migration, and operations impact. A user-interface request may still change authority or state semantics. Changes with breaking meaning return to architecture review.

## Closure

Project closure transfers products, ownership, runbooks, contracts, registries, risk, debt, support, and benefit tracking into normal portfolio operations. Temporary environments, credentials, integrations, and exceptions are retired or governed.
