# Federated Truth Pattern

## Context

No single organization owns all truth in global trade. Customs owns legal determinations; carriers own operational movement facts; banks own settlement facts; traders own commercial intent and obligations within agreement scope.

## Pattern

GTOS establishes proposition-level authority. Each proposition declares owner, scope, jurisdiction, effective time, evidence, and contract. Consumers combine propositions into perspectives while retaining origin and authority.

## Conflict

When authorities disagree, GTOS preserves both claims and identifies the legal or contractual mechanism for resolution. The platform SHALL NOT choose a winner based solely on message arrival time, system priority, or database location.

## Projection

A control-tower view may combine shipment position, customs status, payment condition, and predicted delay. The view is operationally valuable but remains a perspective. It does not become the owner of underlying facts.

## Benefits

Federation reduces reconciliation ambiguity, supports participant autonomy, and permits technology diversity without semantic anarchy.
