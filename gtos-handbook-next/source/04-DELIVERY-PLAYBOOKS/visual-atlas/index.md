# GTOS Visual Architecture Atlas

The Visual Atlas provides a high-level map of the theory, domains, delivery lifecycle, AI runtime, data lineage, security boundaries, deployment, decision chain, and correction flow. Each diagram is explanatory; the normative text and machine-readable registries remain authoritative.

## How to Use the Atlas

Use the diagrams during executive orientation, architecture workshops, domain onboarding, design reviews, engineering onboarding, partner certification, and incident exercises. Every diagram should be read together with its corresponding chapter and scenario.
