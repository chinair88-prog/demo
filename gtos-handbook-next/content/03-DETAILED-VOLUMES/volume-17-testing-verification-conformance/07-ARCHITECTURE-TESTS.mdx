<h2 id="1-executive-orientation">1. Executive Orientation</h2>
<br />
<p>This chapter defines how GTOS shall <strong>define the target architecture, implementation standard, evidence, security, reliability, operations, tests, migration and conformance requirements for Architecture Tests</strong>.</p>
<br />
<p>The specification separates reality, observations, knowledge, Decisions, Intent, execution, and outcomes. A component name, database row, UI label, AI answer, or workflow state does not acquire authority merely because it is visible or technically convenient.</p>
<br />
<CodeBlock code={"Reality or external outcome\n    ≠ Observation\n    ≠ Claim\n    ≠ derived Perspective\n    ≠ Prediction\n    ≠ Recommendation\n    ≠ authorized Decision\n    ≠ execution Intent"} language="text" />
<br />
<h3 id="11-accountable-ownership">1.1 Accountable ownership</h3>
<br />
<p>The accountable owner is <strong>Quality Engineering Council</strong>. Supporting applications, shared infrastructure, integration adapters, analytics, and AI coordinate or present the capability but shall not silently own its authoritative propositions.</p>
<br />
<h3 id="12-scope">1.2 Scope</h3>
<br />
<p>- create and identify the subject</p>
<p>- validate evidence and policy</p>
<p>- make authorized Decisions</p>
<p>- execute typed Commands</p>
<p>- publish completed-fact Events</p>
<p>- query current and historical perspectives</p>
<p>- correct and reconcile outcomes</p>
<br />
<h3 id="13-non-goals">1.3 Non-goals</h3>
<br />
<p>- treating a registered Gradle module, catalog record, README, frontend route, migration plan, or generated report as proof of end-to-end implementation;</p>
<p>- direct cross-Domain database writes;</p>
<p>- generic <code class="code-inline">status</code> values that combine lifecycle, approval, provider, document, payment, dispute, and correction state;</p>
<p>- silent replacement of history;</p>
<p>- AI-created authority or fabricated external outcomes.</p>

<h3 id="21-core-invariants">2.1 Core invariants</h3>
<br />
<p>- Stable identity precedes relationship, state, and cross-system correlation.</p>
<p>- Effective time and recorded time remain distinct where business truth changes over time.</p>
<p>- Recommendation, Decision, Intent, execution, and outcome are separate concepts.</p>
<p>- No Portal, report, search index, cache, workflow, or AI Agent becomes a shadow owner of Domain truth.</p>
<p>- Binding submitted, approved, issued, accepted, or signed versions are immutable.</p>
<p>- Corrections append governed facts and preserve the original record.</p>
<p>- External authority and provider outcomes retain their source and qualification.</p>
<p>- Tenant and represented-principal context come from trusted authentication and mandate evaluation.</p>
<p>- Retried writes are idempotent and concurrency guarded.</p>
<p>- Outcome Unknown is explicit and reconciled before risky duplicate execution.</p>
<br />
<h3 id="22-required-standards">2.2 Required standards</h3>
<br />
<p>- stable identifiers</p>
<p>- typed contracts</p>
<p>- immutable binding versions</p>
<p>- transactional outbox</p>
<p>- idempotent Commands</p>
<p>- explicit Outcome Unknown</p>
<p>- correction lineage</p>
<p>- machine-verifiable conformance</p>

<h2 id="3-actors-roles-and-authority">3. Actors, Roles, and Authority</h2>
<br />
<p>A conformant design distinguishes:</p>
<br />
<p>- the natural person acting;</p>
<p>- the service or AI identity invoking a Tool;</p>
<p>- the organization or principal represented;</p>
<p>- the role or relationship granting context;</p>
<p>- the mandate or delegation granting action authority;</p>
<p>- the Policy and jurisdiction limiting the action;</p>
<p>- the reviewer or approver responsible for a binding Decision.</p>
<br />
<p>Authority is evaluated at action time. Authentication alone does not grant business authority. Cached permissions and browser-supplied tenant headers are insufficient for high-impact actions.</p>

<h3 id="41-transition-rules">4.1 Transition rules</h3>
<br />
<p>- transition through a typed, authorized Command;</p>
<p>- require exact expected version where concurrent change matters;</p>
<p>- record actor, represented principal, mandate, Policy, reason, and Evidence;</p>
<p>- use an idempotency key for retried writes;</p>
<p>- publish a completed-fact Event after durable state change;</p>
<p>- expose Outcome Unknown for unresolved external completion;</p>
<p>- preserve original and corrected states in historical reconstruction.</p>

<h2 id="7-workflow-failure-and-compensation">7. Workflow, Failure, and Compensation</h2>
<br />
<DiagramBlock src="/diagrams/architecture-overview.svg" alt="Mermaid Diagram" caption="Workflow / State Diagram" />
<br />
<p>Representative failure modes:</p>
<br />
<p>- stale or wrong source version</p>
<p>- duplicate submission or replay</p>
<p>- external timeout after possible success</p>
<p>- contradictory source observations</p>
<p>- lost Event after committed state</p>
<p>- approval or mandate revoked during execution</p>
<p>- downstream action before a mandatory gate</p>
<p>- partial completion across multiple subjects</p>
<p>- evidence later retracted or corrected</p>
<p>- tenant, facility, channel or jurisdiction mismatch</p>
<br />
<p>Recovery shall query the authoritative source before duplicate execution, preserve partial success, use compensating Intent rather than destructive rewrite, publish corrections, quarantine high-impact ambiguity, and record affected downstream subjects.</p>

<h2 id="9-portal-and-experience-requirements">9. Portal and Experience Requirements</h2>
<br />
<p>- Primary participant Portal: initiate, review exact versions, supply evidence, and respond to required actions.</p>
<p>- Operator Portal: inspect exceptions, correlation, source responses, retries, compensation, and downstream impact.</p>
<p>- Executive or oversight view: consume governed metrics without becoming a source of Domain truth.</p>

<p>Every Portal displays exact version, owner, state, freshness, required action, and uncertainty. <h2 id="10-ai-participation-and-tool-governance">10. AI Participation and Tool Governance</h2>
<br />
<p>Permitted AI tasks:</p>
<br />
<p>- extract and classify evidence</p>
<p>- detect anomalies and missing fields</p>
<p>- summarize timelines and contradictions</p>
<p>- recommend next actions with sources and uncertainty</p>
<br />
<p>AI shall not own Domain records, grant itself authority, suppress contradictions, silently edit approved versions, fabricate external outcomes, or execute high-impact actions outside typed Tools, policy, approval, idempotency, sandbox, and outcome observation.</p>
<br />
<p>Every Agent Run records source context, prompt/model/tool versions, structured output, confidence and limitations, Policy result, approval, Tool calls, provider responses, final outcome, and recovery actions.</p>

<h2 id="15-worked-conformance-scenario">15. Worked Conformance Scenario</h2>
<br />
<p>An authorized principal initiates a Command against an exact subject version. The service validates identity, tenant, mandate, Policy, Evidence, and idempotency. It records the state transition and outbox entry atomically. If an external dependency participates, timeout produces Outcome Unknown; reconciliation queries the source or waits for a verified callback instead of repeating the action blindly.</p>
<br />
<p>Portals show source, freshness, version, required action, and uncertainty. AI may extract, compare, summarize, detect anomalies, and recommend through typed Tools, but cannot own the subject or fabricate success. Later correction appends new facts, identifies affected projections and workflows, and preserves the historical Decision context.</p>

<h2 id="16-conformance-checklist">16. Conformance Checklist</h2>
<br />

<p>- [ ] Commands are typed, authorized, idempotent, and version guarded;</p>
<p>- [ ] Events describe completed facts;</p>
<p>- [ ] Evidence includes provenance, time, integrity, qualification, and correction;</p>
<p>- [ ] external outcomes retain their authority source;</p>
<p>- [ ] Portals show freshness and uncertainty;</p>
<p>- [ ] AI is bounded by typed Tools, Policy, approval, and outcome observation;</p>
<p>- [ ] security, tenant, facility, channel, and jurisdiction boundaries are enforced;</p>
<p>- [ ] Outcome Unknown and reconciliation exist where needed;</p>
<p>- [ ] corrections preserve history;</p>
<p>- [ ] tests prove invariants and recovery;</p>
<p>- [ ] implementation status is not upgraded without executable evidence.</p>
