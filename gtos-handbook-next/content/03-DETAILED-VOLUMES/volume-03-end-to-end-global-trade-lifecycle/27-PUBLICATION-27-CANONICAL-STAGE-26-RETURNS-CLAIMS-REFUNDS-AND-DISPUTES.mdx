<h2 id="1-executive-orientation">1. Executive Orientation</h2>
<br />
<p>This stage exists to <strong>govern post-delivery remediation through return authorization, reverse logistics, inspection, claims, refunds, chargebacks, and disputes</strong>.</p>
<br />
<p>Its output is not merely a screen state or document download. It is a governed set of identified subjects, Relationships, Decisions, Intent, Commands, completed facts, external observations, evidence, and correction history.</p>
<br />
<CodeBlock code={"Reality or external outcome\n    ≠ platform Observation\n    ≠ derived Perspective\n    ≠ AI Prediction\n    ≠ Recommendation\n    ≠ authorized Decision\n    ≠ execution Intent"} language="text" />
<br />
<h3 id="11-accountability">1.1 Accountability</h3>
<br />
<p>The accountable capability is <strong>Returns / CRM / Payment / Finance</strong>. Supporting Portals, workflow engines, reports, search indexes, integration adapters, and AI Agents may coordinate or present the work, but they do not silently become owners of the underlying business truth.</p>
<br />
<h3 id="12-primary-business-outcomes">1.2 Primary business outcomes</h3>
<br />
<p>- a stable subject identity and exact source-version lineage;</p>
<p>- explicit authority and effective mandate;</p>
<p>- a testable lifecycle outcome;</p>
<p>- evidence sufficient for downstream use and later reconstruction;</p>
<p>- visible failure, partial completion, uncertainty, and correction;</p>
<p>- a completed-fact Event or qualified external observation;</p>
<p>- downstream activation without shared-database ownership transfer.</p>
<br />
<h3 id="13-non-goals">1.3 Non-goals</h3>
<br />
<p>- treating a registered module as proof of complete implementation;</p>
<p>- treating a frontend route as proof of backend behavior;</p>
<p>- allowing an AI recommendation to become a binding Decision;</p>
<p>- inventing external-provider or government-authority success;</p>
<p>- overwriting history during amendment or correction;</p>
<p>- combining unrelated states into one generic <code class="code-inline">status</code> field.</p>
<br />

<h2 id="2-entry-and-exit-criteria">2. Entry and Exit Criteria</h2>
<br />
<h3 id="21-entry-criteria">2.1 Entry criteria</h3>
<br />
<p>1. Every upstream subject reference resolves to a stable identifier.</p>
<p>2. The exact approved, accepted, issued, or observed version is known.</p>
<p>3. The initiating human, service, organization, or AI Agent is authenticated.</p>
<p>4. The represented principal and mandate are valid at command time.</p>
<p>5. Required Evidence exists, or an explicit authorized exception is linked.</p>
<p>6. Applicable Policy and jurisdiction versions are fixed.</p>
<p>7. Duplicate, stale-version, and replay checks have run.</p>
<p>8. Any conflicting active process is resolved, suspended, or explicitly related.</p>
<br />
<h3 id="22-exit-criteria">2.2 Exit criteria</h3>
<br />
<p>1. The authoritative Domain or external authority records the outcome.</p>
<p>2. State and version changes are durable.</p>
<p>3. A completed-fact Event or qualified observation is available.</p>
<p>4. Downstream consumers receive stable subject, correlation, and causation references.</p>
<p>5. Unknown, partial, disputed, or failed outcomes remain visible.</p>
<p>6. Corrections preserve the original record and reason.</p>
<p>7. Metrics, audit evidence, and operational signals are emitted.</p>
<p>8. A qualified reviewer can reconstruct who knew what, when, and why.</p>

<h3 id="31-identity-and-relationship-rules">3.1 Identity and relationship rules</h3>
<br />
<p>- a display name, document number, provider reference, barcode, or URL is not automatically canonical identity;</p>
<p>- relationships such as custody, mandate, allocation, publication, reservation, or obligation are independent facts;</p>
<p>- cross-Domain references use stable IDs and qualified snapshots, not shared-table mutation;</p>
<p>- external identifiers retain issuer and namespace;</p>
<p>- merge, split, correction, and supersession preserve lineage.</p>
<br />
<h3 id="32-invariants">3.2 Invariants</h3>
<br />
<p>1. Submitted, approved, accepted, issued, or signed versions are immutable.</p>
<p>2. State changes occur through authorized Commands.</p>
<p>3. Completed-fact Events use past-tense semantics.</p>
<p>4. A Recommendation cannot self-promote to a Decision.</p>
<p>5. An external outcome retains its source authority.</p>
<p>6. Derived projections cannot become authoritative by repeated copying.</p>
<p>7. All writes are tenant-, organization-, facility-, and authority-scoped as applicable.</p>
<p>8. Retried writes are idempotent.</p>
<p>9. Outcome Unknown is not converted to success or failure by assumption.</p>
<p>10. Corrections reference the corrected fact and downstream impact.</p>

<h3 id="44-forbidden-state-behavior">4.4 Forbidden state behavior</h3>
<br />
<p>- direct arbitrary assignment from a Portal request;</p>
<p>- using one status to represent lifecycle, approval, payment, document, provider, and dispute state simultaneously;</p>
<p>- deleting terminal history to reopen a process;</p>
<p>- inferring completion from elapsed time or an AI prediction;</p>
<p>- moving past a mandatory gate because a downstream system already acted.</p>

<h3 id="decision-rules">Decision rules</h3>
<br />

<p>- authority and delegation are evaluated at Decision time;</p>
<p>- a Policy version and jurisdiction context are recorded;</p>
<p>- mandatory-gate failure blocks progression unless a lawful exception exists;</p>

<p>- Decision correction does not erase the original Decision;</p>

<h2 id="6-evidence-documents-and-knowledge-cutoff">6. Evidence, Documents, and Knowledge Cutoff</h2>
<br />
<p>Every binding or high-impact action requires an Evidence Set containing:</p>
<br />
<p>- evidence identity and integrity hash where required;</p>
<p>- issuing, observing, or signing party;</p>
<p>- observed time, received time, and effective-time interval;</p>
<p>- subject and source-version relationships;</p>
<p>- qualification, confidence, and limitations;</p>
<p>- contradiction, retraction, supersession, and correction links;</p>
<p>- jurisdiction, classification, retention, and residency;</p>
<p>- chain of custody for physical or high-integrity evidence;</p>
<p>- the knowledge cutoff used by the Decision.</p>
<br />
<p>A document is not automatically truth. It is an Artifact containing Claims. Its legal or operational effect depends on issuer, mandate, signature, version, applicable rules, and external authority.</p>

<h2 id="15-failure-modes-and-recovery">15. Failure Modes and Recovery</h2>
<br />
<p>Representative failures:</p>
<br />
<p>- stale or wrong source version;</p>
<p>- duplicate submission or replay;</p>
<p>- external timeout after possible success;</p>
<p>- contradictory source observations;</p>
<p>- lost Event after committed state;</p>
<p>- approval or mandate revoked during execution;</p>
<p>- downstream action performed before a mandatory gate;</p>
<p>- partial completion across multiple subjects;</p>
<p>- evidence later retracted or corrected;</p>
<p>- tenant, facility, or jurisdiction mismatch.</p>
<br />
<p>Recovery principles:</p>
<br />
<p>1. Query the authoritative source before repeating an external action.</p>
<p>2. Preserve partial success.</p>
<p>3. Use compensating Intent instead of destructive history rewrite.</p>
<p>4. Publish correction Events when distributed facts change.</p>
<p>5. Quarantine high-impact ambiguity.</p>
<p>6. Escalate when authority or legal effect is unclear.</p>
<p>7. Record business impact, affected subjects, and final reconciliation.</p>

<h2 id="18-worked-scenario">18. Worked Scenario</h2>
<br />
<p>An upstream governed commitment enters the stage with exact subject and version references. The initiating principal is authenticated, and its mandate is checked at command time. Evidence is gathered and evaluated against the applicable policy version and jurisdiction.</p>
<br />
<p>A recommendation may be produced, but an authorized Decision remains separate. The Domain accepts an idempotent Command and records state plus an outbox entry atomically. When an external party participates, a timeout creates <code class="code-inline">Outcome Unknown</code>; the workflow queries the external source or waits for a verified callback instead of repeating execution blindly.</p>
<br />
<p>On success or failure, the system records a completed fact, publishes a stable Event, updates downstream projections, and preserves source and knowledge cutoff. Later corrections append new facts and identify downstream impact rather than replacing history. AI assists through typed tools and cannot own the subject, grant authority, or invent an outcome.</p>
