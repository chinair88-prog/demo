<br />
<h2 id="verified-implementation">Verified implementation</h2>
<br />
<p><code class="code-inline">PaymentIntentEntity</code> maps to <code class="code-inline">payment_service_intents</code> and defines indexes for order, status, provider, customer, creation, expiry, and tenant. It records amount, currency, capture mode, status, provider, provider reference, customer, description, expiry, errors, metadata, a unique idempotency key, and a JPA optimistic-lock version.</p>
<br />
<p>Lifecycle methods support captured, partially captured, cancelled, expired, and failed states. Captured intents cannot be cancelled.</p>
<br />
<p><code class="code-inline">PaymentIntentService</code> implements creation, idempotency lookup, tenant-aware queries, pagination, provider/customer/status filters, and cancellation. The service obtains tenant context from a gateway-injected header and uses unscoped behavior when the context is absent for internal flows.</p>
<br />
<p><code class="code-inline">PaymentController</code> exposes payment intents, capture, refund, settlement, payout, webhook, dispute, summary, and provider information under <code class="code-inline">/api/payments</code>.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="verified-gaps-and-risks">Verified gaps and risks</h2>
<br />
<p>- Legacy/internal unscoped access is deliberately preserved and requires strong trusted-caller controls.</p>
<p>- Tenant context depends on a gateway-injected header; direct-service access must be prevented or independently authenticated.</p>
<p>- Idempotency lookup is by idempotency key without an inspected tenant predicate; a global unique key may cause cross-tenant behavior unless keys are globally namespaced and responses are protected.</p>
<p>- The inspected payment-intent status model starts at <code class="code-inline">REQUIRES_CAPTURE</code>; separate authorization, risk approval, and Outcome Unknown states are not part of this Entity.</p>
<p>- No formal PI risk-approval Decision aggregate was established in the inspected files.</p>
<p>- Webhook signature verification and provider-outcome reconciliation require inspection of additional classes.</p>
