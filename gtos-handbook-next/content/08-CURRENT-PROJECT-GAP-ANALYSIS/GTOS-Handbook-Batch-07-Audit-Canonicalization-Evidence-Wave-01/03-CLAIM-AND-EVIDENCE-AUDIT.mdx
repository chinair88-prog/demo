<h2 id="required-upgrade-evidence">Required upgrade evidence</h2>
<br />
<p>A chapter may be upgraded from <code class="code-inline">TEMPLATE_DERIVED_REFERENCE_SKELETON</code> only after it identifies, at minimum:</p>
<br />
<p>- exact repository paths and verified commit;</p>
<p>- entities, aggregates, state fields, and lifecycle methods;</p>
<p>- controllers, route methods, request/response types, and authorization;</p>
<p>- migrations, table names, keys, indexes, and ownership;</p>
<p>- Events, outbox, consumers, replay, and workflow links;</p>
<p>- Portal routes and client contracts;</p>
<p>- tests proving positive, negative, concurrency, tenant, security, and recovery behavior;</p>
<p>- deployment and operational evidence where relevant;</p>
<p>- specific gaps and contradictions.</p>
