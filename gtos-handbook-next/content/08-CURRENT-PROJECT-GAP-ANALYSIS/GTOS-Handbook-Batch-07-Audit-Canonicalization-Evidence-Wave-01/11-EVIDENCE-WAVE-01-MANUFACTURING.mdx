<h2 id="verified-implementation">Verified implementation</h2>
<br />
<p>The manufacturing Entity maps to <code class="code-inline">manufacturing.tb_production_order</code>. It records production-order number, purchase order, supplier, product, quantity, unit, planned and actual dates, status, priority, notes, company, and timestamps.</p>
<br />
<p>The service implements:</p>
<br />
<p>- production-order list/get/create/update/delete;</p>
<p>- production materials with required, purchased, and received quantities;</p>
<p>- production progress with stage, percentage, completed and defective quantity, images, videos, reporter, and time;</p>
<p>- production schedules with line, date, shift, planned output, actual output, and status.</p>
<br />
<p>The controller exposes production-order, material, progress, and schedule routes under <code class="code-inline">/api/manufacturing/production-orders</code>.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="verified-gaps">Verified gaps</h2>
<br />
<p>- No tenant field exists on the inspected production-order Entity; company ID is supplied in requests.</p>
<p>- Controller and service methods shown do not establish authorization or company-scope validation.</p>
<p>- Full update permits arbitrary status and field replacement without transition guards.</p>
<p>- Delete removes materials, progress, schedules, and the order, conflicting with history-preservation principles.</p>
<p>- No optimistic lock/version field was found.</p>
<p>- No Events or transactional outbox were found in the inspected service.</p>
<p>- Capacity commitment, factory qualification, BOM/routing, work orders, lot genealogy, quality hold points, and immutable baseline/rebaseline are not modeled in the inspected files.</p>
<p>- A second Production Order implementation under Warehouse indicates an ownership conflict.</p>
