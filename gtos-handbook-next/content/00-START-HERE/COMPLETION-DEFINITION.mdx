<br />
<p>A GTOS capability is complete only when every required question below has an evidence-backed answer.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="business-and-ownership">Business and ownership</h2>
<br />
<p>- What business outcome does the capability produce?</p>
<p>- Which bounded context owns each proposition?</p>
<p>- Which external authority owns legal, financial, quality, carrier, or regulatory outcomes?</p>
<p>- Who may decide, approve, execute, correct, revoke, and appeal?</p>
<br />
<h2 id="domain-model">Domain model</h2>
<br />
<p>- What are the canonical subjects and stable identities?</p>
<p>- What are the aggregates, entities, value objects, relationships, and invariants?</p>
<p>- What state machines exist and what guards every transition?</p>
<p>- What history, correction, supersession, contradiction, and dispute semantics apply?</p>
<br />
<h2 id="contracts-and-data">Contracts and data</h2>
<br />
<p>- What Commands, Events, queries, APIs, webhooks, files, and tools exist?</p>
<p>- Which service owns each database, schema, table, index, and migration?</p>
<p>- How are idempotency, concurrency, ordering, replay, reconciliation, and versioning handled?</p>
<p>- How are lineage, classification, retention, residency, and deletion handled?</p>
<br />
<h2 id="experience-and-automation">Experience and automation</h2>
<br />
<p>- Which personas and Portals participate?</p>
<p>- How are partial, stale, denied, failed, disputed, and outcome-unknown states displayed?</p>
<p>- What AI tasks are allowed, what autonomy level applies, and what human approval is required?</p>
<p>- What evidence and explanation accompany automated recommendations and actions?</p>
<br />
<h2 id="quality-and-operations">Quality and operations</h2>
<br />
<p>- What security and privacy controls are enforced?</p>
<p>- What SLOs, dashboards, alerts, runbooks, capacity limits, and recovery objectives exist?</p>
<p>- What unit, property, integration, contract, security, performance, chaos, migration, E2E, and DR tests prove the behavior?</p>
<p>- What release evidence demonstrates deployability and operability?</p>
<br />
<p>A module name, route, table, mock screen, plan, or generated document is never sufficient evidence by itself.</p>
