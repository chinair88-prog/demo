<h2 id="context">Context</h2>
<br />
<p>No single organization owns all truth in global trade. Customs owns legal determinations; carriers own operational movement facts; banks own settlement facts; traders own commercial intent and obligations within agreement scope.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="pattern">Pattern</h2>
<br />
<p>GTOS establishes proposition-level authority. Each proposition declares owner, scope, jurisdiction, effective time, evidence, and contract. Consumers combine propositions into perspectives while retaining origin and authority.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="conflict">Conflict</h2>
<br />
<p>When authorities disagree, GTOS preserves both claims and identifies the legal or contractual mechanism for resolution. The platform SHALL NOT choose a winner based solely on message arrival time, system priority, or database location.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="projection">Projection</h2>
<br />
<p>A control-tower view may combine shipment position, customs status, payment condition, and predicted delay. The view is operationally valuable but remains a perspective. It does not become the owner of underlying facts.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="benefits">Benefits</h2>
<br />
<p>Federation reduces reconciliation ambiguity, supports participant autonomy, and permits technology diversity without semantic anarchy.</p>
