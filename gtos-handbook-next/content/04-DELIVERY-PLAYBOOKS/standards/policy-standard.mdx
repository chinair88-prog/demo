<h2 id="purpose">Purpose</h2>
<br />
<p>Policy expresses permission, prohibition, obligation, required approval, threshold, routing, or evidence requirement.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="policy-anatomy">Policy Anatomy</h2>
<br />
<p>A policy has identity, owner, authority basis, scope, effective time, version, inputs, rule logic, result types, explanation template, tests, and retirement state.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="input-context">Input Context</h2>
<br />
<p>Inputs may include actor identity, organizational Relationship, delegation, resource, action, purpose, jurisdiction, time, classification, risk, evidence, and operational state.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="results">Results</h2>
<br />
<p>A result includes permit, deny, require approval, require evidence, or permit with obligations. The result records policy version, material inputs, reason codes, and decision time.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="separation">Separation</h2>
<br />
<p>Policy evaluation is not always a business Decision. A policy may provide a required control used by an authorized Decision process. Where policy automatically produces a binding determination, the evaluation is recorded as a Decision with authority.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="testing">Testing</h2>
<br />
<p>Policies require positive, negative, boundary, temporal, jurisdiction, delegation, and conflict tests. Production policy changes use staged rollout and decision comparison.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="conflict-resolution">Conflict Resolution</h2>
<br />
<p>Conflicts are resolved by explicit priority and authority, not rule order accident. More restrictive rules do not automatically win when jurisdictions or legal authorities differ; the applicable authority model determines precedence.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="explainability">Explainability</h2>
<br />
<p>Explanations identify the governing rule and material facts without exposing secrets or enabling control evasion.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="lifecycle">Lifecycle</h2>
<br />
<p>Policies are reviewed when law, contract, risk, business model, or evidence changes. Expired policy SHALL not remain silently active.</p>
