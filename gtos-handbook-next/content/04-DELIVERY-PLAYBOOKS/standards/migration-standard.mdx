<h2 id="scope">Scope</h2>
<br />
<p>Migration includes database change, service extraction, platform replacement, partner transition, schema evolution, identity merge, policy change, and model upgrade.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="required-plan">Required Plan</h2>
<br />
<p>A migration plan defines source and target semantics, ownership, mapping, temporal treatment, correction behavior, compatibility, validation, cutover, rollback, reconciliation, and retirement.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="dual-operation">Dual Operation</h2>
<br />
<p>During dual run, one truth owner remains explicit. Dual writes require deterministic conflict handling and reconciliation. Comparing record counts is insufficient; propositions and outcomes are compared.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="historical-data">Historical Data</h2>
<br />
<p>Historical data retains original identifiers, source, time, and meaning. Transformations record lineage. Present-day rules SHALL not silently reinterpret historical facts.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="cutover">Cutover</h2>
<br />
<p>Cutover criteria include data completeness, invariant validity, consumer readiness, operational telemetry, rollback window, and unresolved exceptions.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="ai-and-analytics">AI and Analytics</h2>
<br />
<p>Feature and model migrations require point-in-time validation and decision comparison. Vector embeddings or summaries SHALL not become the only retained representation of evidence.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="completion">Completion</h2>
<br />
<p>Migration completes only after old write paths are disabled, consumers are migrated, evidence is archived, reconciliation passes, and ownership is updated.</p>
