<br />
<h2 id="assessment-scope">Assessment Scope</h2>
<br />
<p>An assessment declares organization, product, domains, environments, version, jurisdictions, and exclusions.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="evidence-method">Evidence Method</h2>
<br />
<p>Assessors evaluate documents, registries, schemas, code, tests, configurations, operational records, incidents, and interviews. Statements without evidence do not establish conformance.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="finding-classes">Finding Classes</h2>
<br />
<p>- conforming;</p>
<p>- observation;</p>
<p>- minor non-conformance;</p>
<p>- major non-conformance;</p>
<p>- critical non-conformance;</p>
<p>- approved exception.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="critical-findings">Critical Findings</h2>
<br />
<p>Critical findings include unowned authoritative truth, unauthorized automated execution, erased governed history, material security bypass, inability to reconstruct high-impact decisions, or false claims of regulatory authority.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="remediation">Remediation</h2>
<br />
<p>Each finding has owner, impact, root condition, remediation, compensating control, deadline, and verification. Closure requires evidence.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="certification-boundary">Certification Boundary</h2>
<br />
<p>Conformance applies only to the assessed scope and version. A compliant platform does not make every deployed extension compliant.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="continuous-conformance">Continuous Conformance</h2>
<br />
<p>Automated checks monitor schemas, registry completeness, policy tests, dependency status, traceability, and operational controls. Periodic review covers semantic and governance concerns not fully automatable.</p>
