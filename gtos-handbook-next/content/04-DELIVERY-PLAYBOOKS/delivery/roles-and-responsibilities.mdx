<h2 id="accountability-rule">Accountability Rule</h2>
<br />
<p>Approval is not attendance. An approver confirms that the artifact is correct within their authority and that unresolved risks are explicit. One person MAY hold multiple roles in a small implementation, but conflicting duties and high-impact approvals SHOULD remain independently reviewed.</p>
