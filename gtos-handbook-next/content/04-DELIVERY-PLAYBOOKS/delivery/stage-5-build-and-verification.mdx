<h2 id="objective">Objective</h2>
<br />
<p>Implement through small vertical slices with invariant, contract, policy, security, resilience, and operational testing.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="entry-conditions">Entry Conditions</h2>
<br />
<p>The preceding stage has approved evidence, open risks are visible, accountable roles are assigned, and the scope is versioned. Urgent delivery MAY begin earlier only through an explicit exception with compensating controls.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="required-work">Required Work</h2>
<br />
<p>1. Confirm business outcome and affected value streams.</p>
<p>2. Identify Canon and constitutional dependencies.</p>
<p>3. Resolve truth ownership, identity, time, state, authority, and evidence questions.</p>
<p>4. Produce the stage artifacts and validate them with downstream teams.</p>
<p>5. Record consequential decisions and rejected alternatives.</p>
<p>6. Evaluate security, privacy, AI, resilience, migration, and operations implications.</p>
<p>7. Demonstrate the completion criteria through evidence.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="mandatory-artifacts">Mandatory Artifacts</h2>
<br />
<p>- working vertical slices</p>
<p>- test evidence</p>
<p>- deployment artifacts</p>
<p>- observability dashboards</p>
<p>- architecture decision records</p>

<h2 id="stop-conditions">Stop Conditions</h2>
<br />
<p>- <strong>STOP:</strong> Testing focuses only on code paths.</p>
<p>- <strong>STOP:</strong> Telemetry lacks business meaning.</p>
<p>- <strong>STOP:</strong> Architecture deviations are undocumented.</p>
<br />
<br />
<h2 id="exit-criteria">Exit Criteria</h2>
<br />
<p>- all mandatory artifacts are approved or have explicit exceptions;</p>
<p>- high-impact risks have owners and controls;</p>
<p>- traceability to Canon is recorded;</p>
<p>- delivery teams understand the next-stage obligations;</p>
<p>- no critical semantic, authority, security, or recovery uncertainty remains hidden.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="accountable-roles">Accountable Roles</h2>
<br />
<p>The stage owner coordinates completion. Domain owners approve meaning and truth. Security, data, AI, and operations approve their control areas. The Architecture Council resolves cross-domain conflicts.</p>
