<h2 id="weekly-domain-review">Weekly Domain Review</h2>
<br />
<p>Review domain decisions, new terms, invariant changes, contract changes, corrections, and consumer issues. The Domain Owner and Architect ensure meaning remains coherent.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="contract-review">Contract Review</h2>
<br />
<p>Producers and material consumers examine API, Event, and data evolution. The review focuses on semantic compatibility, time, authority, retry, and migration. Schema diffs are supporting evidence rather than the whole review.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="decision-and-ai-review">Decision and AI Review</h2>
<br />
<p>For automated or AI-assisted decision classes, review evidence quality, model and policy versions, escalation, reversal, appeals, outcome quality, drift, security signals, and containment readiness.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="operational-review">Operational Review</h2>
<br />
<p>Review SLOs, error budgets, incidents, correction backlog, Event freshness, partner health, capacity, vulnerabilities, and runbook actions. Business and technical signals appear together.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="architecture-council">Architecture Council</h2>
<br />
<p>The council resolves cross-domain ownership, constitutional exceptions, major ADRs, shared standards, and debt. It does not micromanage local implementation choices that conform to the architecture.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="retrospective">Retrospective</h2>
<br />
<p>Retrospectives identify conditions in process, tooling, architecture, and organization. Actions have owners and evidence. They do not rewrite the history of decisions or blame individuals for predictable system conditions.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="portfolio-review">Portfolio Review</h2>
<br />
<p>Executives review value realization, adoption, foundation investment, domain roadmap, major risk, partner scale, and decommission progress. Teams demonstrate business outcomes and conformance rather than only feature counts.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="ceremony-discipline">Ceremony Discipline</h2>
<br />
<p>Every recurring ceremony has purpose, inputs, decisions, outputs, participants, and cancellation criteria. Meetings without owned outcomes are removed. Decisions are captured in durable artifacts rather than remaining in conversation.</p>
