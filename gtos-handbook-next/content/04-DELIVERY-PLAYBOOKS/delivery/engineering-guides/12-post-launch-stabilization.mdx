<h2 id="stabilization-window">Stabilization Window</h2>
<br />
<p>A defined stabilization period follows launch or major migration. Change volume is controlled, monitoring is heightened, and the delivery team remains connected to operations and domain owners.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="daily-review">Daily Review</h2>
<br />
<p>Review SLOs, business completion, Command rejection, Event lag, data reconciliation, policy denial, partner incidents, evidence gaps, security signals, AI outcomes, user feedback, and support cases. Metrics are compared with launch assumptions.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="defect-classification">Defect Classification</h2>
<br />
<p>Distinguish semantic defects, contract defects, data defects, policy defects, security defects, operational defects, usability issues, and training gaps. A symptom such as “wrong status” may originate in ownership, temporal projection, late Event, or UI language.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="decision-discipline">Decision Discipline</h2>
<br />
<p>Urgent fixes still record authority and evidence. Temporary bypasses have scope, owner, expiry, and detection. Stabilization SHALL not normalize manual edits to authoritative stores.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="reconciliation">Reconciliation</h2>
<br />
<p>High-impact domains perform daily or continuous reconciliation of identities, obligations, shipments, decisions, settlements, and Event positions. Differences are investigated through lineage rather than overwritten.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="transition-to-normal-operations">Transition to Normal Operations</h2>
<br />
<p>Exit criteria include sustained SLOs, reconciled data, manageable incident and defect rate, trained support, closed critical risks, stable partner behavior, verified runbooks, and accepted ownership. Remaining work transfers to a visible backlog.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="learning">Learning</h2>
<br />
<p>The team updates architecture patterns, tests, capacity assumptions, onboarding material, and delivery gates based on observed behavior. Benefits are measured against the business baseline.</p>
