<p>The Delivery Handbook is written for program leaders, project managers, product owners, architects, engineers, analysts, security teams, data teams, AI teams, and operations. It converts the architecture into stage gates, work products, review questions, and completion evidence.</p>
<br />
<p>A stage is complete only when the required architectural evidence exists. Presentation decks without inspectable models, contracts, tests, and accountable ownership do not satisfy the gate.</p>
