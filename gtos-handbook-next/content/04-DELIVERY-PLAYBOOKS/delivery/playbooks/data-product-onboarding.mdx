<br />
<h2 id="purpose">Purpose</h2>
<br />
<p>Publish governed operational or analytical data for reuse.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="when-to-use">When to Use</h2>
<br />
<p>Use this playbook when creating or materially changing a GTOS domain, platform capability, integration, data product, AI capability, security control, migration, or operational process. The facilitator SHALL adapt detail to impact without removing foundational questions.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="preparation">Preparation</h2>
<br />
<p>Identify sponsor, accountable owner, participants, decisions required, source evidence, current constraints, affected jurisdictions, and the deadline. Distribute existing domain models, contracts, incidents, policies, and metrics before the session.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="working-steps">Working Steps</h2>
<br />
<p>1. Declare owner and consumers.</p>
<p>2. Define grain and semantics.</p>
<p>3. Publish schema and quality rules.</p>
<p>4. Add lineage and classification.</p>
<p>5. Test point-in-time correctness.</p>
<p>6. Define deprecation.</p>
<br />
<br />
<h2 id="facilitation-questions">Facilitation Questions</h2>
<br />
<p>- What exists in business reality independently of our software?</p>
<p>- Who owns each proposition and under what authority?</p>
<p>- Which identifiers remain stable through change?</p>
<p>- Which time dimensions matter?</p>
<p>- What state and invariants must be protected?</p>
<p>- Which messages are Commands, Events, Observations, Recommendations, or Decisions?</p>
<p>- What evidence is available and what remains uncertain?</p>
<p>- What Intent authorizes execution?</p>
<p>- How are correction, replay, recovery, and dispute handled?</p>
<br />
<h2 id="outputs">Outputs</h2>
<br />
<p>- data contract</p>
<p>- quality SLOs</p>
<p>- lineage graph</p>
<p>- access policy</p>
<p>- consumer registry</p>
<br />
<br />
<h2 id="quality-controls">Quality Controls</h2>
<br />
<p>Outputs SHALL use GTOS terminology, identify Canon dependencies, distinguish authority from technical ownership, and include unresolved questions. Decisions made during the playbook are captured in ADRs or governed registries.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="completion-evidence">Completion Evidence</h2>
<br />
<p>The playbook is complete when accountable owners approve the outputs, downstream teams can implement from them, testable acceptance criteria exist, and critical uncertainty is either resolved or formally controlled.</p>
