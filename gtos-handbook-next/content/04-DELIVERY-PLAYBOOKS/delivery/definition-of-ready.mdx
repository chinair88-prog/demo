<br />
<p>A work item is ready for implementation only when:</p>
<br />
<p>- the business outcome and affected subject are explicit;</p>
<p>- truth ownership is known;</p>
<p>- required identity and temporal semantics are defined;</p>
<p>- state, invariants, Command, Event, and Decision behavior are understood;</p>
<p>- contracts and acceptance criteria exist;</p>
<p>- security classification and authority are defined;</p>
<p>- AI involvement, if any, has an approved output type and mandate;</p>
<p>- migration and compatibility impact are known;</p>
<p>- observability and operational expectations are included;</p>
<p>- open questions have owners and dates.</p>
<br />
<p>A backlog item that says only “build API,” “add AI,” or “sync data” is not ready.</p>
