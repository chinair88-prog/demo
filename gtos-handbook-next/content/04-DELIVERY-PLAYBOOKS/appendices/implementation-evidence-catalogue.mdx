<br />
<h2 id="architecture-evidence">Architecture Evidence</h2>
<br />
<p>- context and container diagrams;</p>
<p>- domain ownership map;</p>
<p>- command and event catalogues;</p>
<p>- state models and invariants;</p>
<p>- Canon traceability matrix;</p>
<p>- ADRs and exception records.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="engineering-evidence">Engineering Evidence</h2>
<br />
<p>- schema validation;</p>
<p>- contract test reports;</p>
<p>- policy tests;</p>
<p>- migration tests;</p>
<p>- security scans;</p>
<p>- dependency and artifact provenance;</p>
<p>- resilience tests.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="operational-evidence">Operational Evidence</h2>
<br />
<p>- SLO reports;</p>
<p>- incident records;</p>
<p>- restoration tests;</p>
<p>- recovery reconciliation;</p>
<p>- access and decision audit;</p>
<p>- AI evaluation and drift reports;</p>
<p>- unresolved risk and exception register.</p>
<br />
<p>Evidence SHALL identify version, scope, owner, collection time, and integrity.</p>
