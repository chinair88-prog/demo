<p>- Canon dependencies identified</p>
<p>- Truth ownership explicit</p>
<p>- Identity and time semantics explicit</p>
<p>- State and Event semantics valid</p>
<p>- Observations separated from authoritative facts</p>
<p>- Decision authority and evidence defined</p>
<p>- Intent precedes execution</p>
<p>- Security, privacy, resilience, and operations included</p>
<p>- Migration and correction behavior defined</p>
<p>- Conformance evidence available</p>
