<h2 id="level-1-semantic">Level 1 — Semantic</h2>
<br />
<p>Uses GTOS terminology and preserves foundational distinctions.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="level-2-architectural">Level 2 — Architectural</h2>
<br />
<p>Implements ownership, contracts, temporal semantics, authority, security, and evidence.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="level-3-operational">Level 3 — Operational</h2>
<br />
<p>Demonstrates SLOs, recovery, incident evidence, policy enforcement, and continuing validation.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="level-4-federated">Level 4 — Federated</h2>
<br />
<p>Interoperates across organizations while preserving authority and jurisdiction.</p>
