<h2 id="gate-1-semantic-integrity">Gate 1 — Semantic Integrity</h2>
<br />
<p>All business concepts use glossary definitions. Ownership, identity, time, state, and relationship semantics are explicit. Commands and Events are distinct.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="gate-2-authority-and-decision">Gate 2 — Authority and Decision</h2>
<br />
<p>Decision classes identify authority, evidence, policy, escalation, appeal, and expected outcome. AI outputs are typed. Execution traces to Intent.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="gate-3-data-and-history">Gate 3 — Data and History</h2>
<br />
<p>Authoritative and derived data are distinguishable. Temporal semantics, lineage, quality, retention, correction, and reconstruction are testable.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="gate-4-security-and-privacy">Gate 4 — Security and Privacy</h2>
<br />
<p>Identity, delegation, purpose, jurisdiction, classification, authorization, audit, and threat controls are complete.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="gate-5-resilience-and-operations">Gate 5 — Resilience and Operations</h2>
<br />
<p>SLO, RTO, RPO, degradation, replay, reconciliation, runbooks, incident evidence, and recovery exercises exist.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="gate-6-change-safety">Gate 6 — Change Safety</h2>
<br />
<p>Contracts, compatibility, migration, rollback, observability, and deprecation are defined.</p>
<br />
<p>A release failing a mandatory Gate SHALL not claim architectural conformance.</p>
