<br />
<h2 id="context">Context</h2>
<br />
<p>Multiple orders from different sellers are consolidated into cargo units, transported across legs, deconsolidated, and delivered to multiple buyers.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="modeling">Modeling</h2>
<br />
<p>Orders, shipments, consignments, cargo units, transport legs, and custody Relationships retain independent identity. A container is not the shipment, and a shipment is not the transport service.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="events">Events</h2>
<br />
<p><code class="code-inline">CargoUnitPacked</code>, <code class="code-inline">ShipmentConsolidated</code>, <code class="code-inline">CustodyTransferred</code>, <code class="code-inline">TransportDeparted</code>, <code class="code-inline">TransportArrived</code>, and <code class="code-inline">ShipmentDelivered</code> describe distinct facts. Schedule updates are not actual movement events.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="state">State</h2>
<br />
<p>A consolidated shipment may contain one held consignment and several releasable consignments. State is scoped; one universal status would either block too much or release too much.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="recovery">Recovery</h2>
<br />
<p>If a message is duplicated, domain invariants and event identity prevent duplicate custody transfer. If events arrive out of order, occurrence time and sequence scope permit reconstruction while preserving receipt order.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="analytics">Analytics</h2>
<br />
<p>A control tower projects end-to-end progress but does not own order, customs, payment, or physical movement truth.</p>
