<br />
<h2 id="context">Context</h2>
<br />
<p>Trade participants and beneficial owners are screened against external lists that change over time and contain uncertain identity information.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="knowledge-discipline">Knowledge Discipline</h2>
<br />
<p>A screening match is a Claim or risk signal, not proof that the party is sanctioned. The system preserves list edition, matching method, identifiers, confidence, and analyst evidence.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="decision">Decision</h2>
<br />
<p>Policy defines thresholds for automatic clear, evidence request, human review, or prohibition. A binding decision requires authority and records the knowledge cutoff.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="temporal-behavior">Temporal Behavior</h2>
<br />
<p>A party may be clear when an order is accepted and later listed before shipment. GTOS distinguishes decision time, policy effective time, and execution time. Controls may require re-screening at defined lifecycle points.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="correction">Correction</h2>
<br />
<p>False-positive resolution records evidence and authority. It does not remove the fact that a match occurred and influenced earlier operations.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="ai">AI</h2>
<br />
<p>AI may assist entity resolution and evidence summarization, but SHALL not promote probabilistic similarity into legal identity without the approved decision path.</p>
