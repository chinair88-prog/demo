<h2 id="core-commercial-domains">Core commercial domains</h2>
<br />
<p>- <strong>Product Catalog</strong> owns canonical product identity and versioned product content.</p>
<p>- <strong>Marketplace</strong> owns seller/channel offers and publication eligibility.</p>
<p>- <strong>Pricing</strong> owns Price Versions and pricing policies.</p>
<p>- <strong>Cart and Checkout</strong> own shopping intent and authoritative checkout quotes.</p>
<p>- <strong>Order</strong> owns accepted customer commercial obligations.</p>
<p>- <strong>Payment</strong> owns Payment Intent and provider financial outcomes.</p>
<p>- <strong>Settlement</strong> owns financial entitlements, reconciliation, payout Intent, and settlement state.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="trade-execution-domains">Trade execution domains</h2>
<br />
<p>- <strong>Procurement</strong> owns RFQ, supplier invitations, and Purchase Orders.</p>
<p>- <strong>Quotation</strong> owns supplier commercial proposals and versions.</p>
<p>- <strong>Contract</strong> owns negotiated binding terms.</p>
<p>- <strong>Trade Documents</strong> owns Proforma Invoice, Packing List, Commercial Invoice, certificates, and document versions.</p>
<p>- <strong>Manufacturing</strong> owns production plans, orders, lots, progress, and capacity commitments.</p>
<p>- <strong>Quality</strong> owns inspection, measurements, nonconformance, and quality-release Decisions.</p>
<p>- <strong>Regulatory and Customs</strong> own platform declarations and authority observations.</p>
<p>- <strong>Shipping</strong> owns shipment, legs, booking relationships, transport observations, and exceptions.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="fulfilment-domains">Fulfilment domains</h2>
<br />
<p>- <strong>Warehouse</strong> owns physical receiving, storage operations, pick, pack, and dispatch handover.</p>
<p>- <strong>Inventory</strong> owns stock positions, reservations, allocations, movements, and adjustments.</p>
<p>- <strong>Fulfilment</strong> coordinates accepted demand to physical execution without taking ownership of Order or Inventory truth.</p>
<p>- <strong>Delivery</strong> owns last-mile attempts and qualified Proof of Delivery observations.</p>
<p>- <strong>Returns</strong> owns return authorization, reverse logistics, inspection, disposition, claims, and disputes.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="supporting-domains">Supporting domains</h2>
<br />
<p>- Identity, Organization, Tenant, Customer, Supplier, Provider, CRM, Communications, Reporting, ESG, and AI Evaluation.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="ownership-rule">Ownership rule</h2>
<br />
<p>A shared concept may appear in several contexts as a reference or projection. Only one context owns each authoritative proposition. Ownership is recorded in the Domain Catalogue and enforced by contracts and database boundaries.</p>
