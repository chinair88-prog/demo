<h2 id="1-truth-has-an-owner">1. Truth has an owner</h2>
<br />
<p>Every proposition has exactly one authoritative owner at a given level of abstraction. Other services may cache or project it but may not mutate it by shared-table access.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="2-history-is-append-oriented">2. History is append-oriented</h2>
<br />
<p>Approved, issued, submitted, signed, paid, released, inspected, delivered, and settled facts shall not be rewritten. Corrections and revocations create new facts linked to the originals.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="3-authority-is-evaluated-at-action-time">3. Authority is evaluated at action time</h2>
<br />
<p>Authentication alone is insufficient. Every binding action must evaluate principal, represented organization, role, relationship, delegation, mandate, jurisdiction, purpose, and policy.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="4-distributed-execution-assumes-partial-failure">4. Distributed execution assumes partial failure</h2>
<br />
<p>Every external or cross-service action must support idempotency, timeouts, retries, duplicate messages, out-of-order delivery, replay, outcome unknown, reconciliation, and compensation.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="5-events-describe-completed-facts">5. Events describe completed facts</h2>
<br />
<p>Commands use imperative names. Events use past-tense completed-fact names. Plans, requests, and predictions are not Events.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="6-ai-is-governed-software">6. AI is governed software</h2>
<br />
<p>AI may extract, classify, summarize, predict, recommend, plan, or invoke typed tools within policy. It shall not own canonical truth, invent authority, conceal uncertainty, or bypass approval.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="7-security-is-architectural">7. Security is architectural</h2>
<br />
<p>Tenant isolation, least privilege, data minimization, secret isolation, integrity, audit, and incident containment are designed into every Domain and contract.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="8-operational-ownership-is-part-of-design">8. Operational ownership is part of design</h2>
<br />
<p>Every service and workflow has an owner, SLO, dashboard, alert, runbook, capacity plan, dependency map, recovery procedure, and decommissioning plan.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="9-compatibility-is-explicit">9. Compatibility is explicit</h2>
<br />
<p>APIs, Events, database migrations, files, tools, prompts, and policy versions evolve through documented compatibility rules.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="10-completion-requires-evidence">10. Completion requires evidence</h2>
<br />
<p>A capability is not complete because code compiles. It is complete when invariants, security, interoperability, recovery, performance, and business outcomes are proven by executable evidence.</p>
