<br />
<p>A capability may enter production only when:</p>
<br />
<p>- ownership and on-call are assigned;</p>
<p>- threat model and privacy review are complete;</p>
<p>- authentication, authorization, tenant isolation, rate limiting, and audit are tested;</p>
<p>- migrations are upgrade-tested and rollback/backout is defined;</p>
<p>- idempotency and concurrency behavior are proven;</p>
<p>- Events and workflows survive duplicate, loss, delay, restart, and replay;</p>
<p>- dependencies have timeouts, circuit breakers, and fallback/reconciliation;</p>
<p>- SLOs, dashboards, alerts, runbooks, and capacity exist;</p>
<p>- backup and restore have been exercised;</p>
<p>- performance and failure tests meet thresholds;</p>
<p>- Portal E2E and accessibility pass;</p>
<p>- AI evaluations and kill switches pass where AI is used;</p>
<p>- release, rollback, and communication plans are approved;</p>
<p>- known risks and exceptions are recorded with expiry.</p>
<br />
<p>Production readiness is a joint Decision by the Domain owner, platform owner, security, SRE, and relevant business authority.</p>
