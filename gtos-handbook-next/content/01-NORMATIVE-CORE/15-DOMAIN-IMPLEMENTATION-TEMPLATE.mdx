<br />
<p>Every Domain pack shall include:</p>
<br />
<p>1. Purpose, scope, owner, external authorities, and non-goals.</p>
<p>2. Ubiquitous language and subject identities.</p>
<p>3. Context map and upstream/downstream relationships.</p>
<p>4. Aggregates, entities, value objects, invariants, and state machines.</p>
<p>5. Decisions, authority, policies, exceptions, appeals, and corrections.</p>
<p>6. Commands, Events, queries, API, webhook, file, and tool contracts.</p>
<p>7. Durable workflows, deadlines, retries, idempotency, reconciliation, and compensation.</p>
<p>8. Database schema, keys, constraints, indexes, versions, migrations, lineage, and retention.</p>
<p>9. Portal journeys, personas, accessibility, localization, and error states.</p>
<p>10. AI use cases, autonomy, context, tools, evaluation, and prohibited behavior.</p>
<p>11. Security, privacy, threat model, tenant isolation, and audit.</p>
<p>12. SLOs, dashboards, alerts, runbooks, capacity, backup, and recovery.</p>
<p>13. Tests, conformance evidence, rollout, migration, compatibility, and deprecation.</p>
<p>14. Worked scenarios including success, partial failure, contradiction, correction, and dispute.</p>
<br />
<p>A Domain review is rejected if any section is replaced by “TBD” without an owner, due date, and risk decision.</p>
