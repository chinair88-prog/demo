<h2 id="ai-control-plane">AI control plane</h2>
<br />
<p>GTOS AI consists of:</p>
<br />
<p>- AI Gateway and provider abstraction;</p>
<p>- Model Router and policy-aware selection;</p>
<p>- Context Builder and governed retrieval;</p>
<p>- Prompt and instruction registry;</p>
<p>- Agent Runtime and state machine;</p>
<p>- Planner, critic, verifier, and supervisor;</p>
<p>- Tool Registry and execution gateway;</p>
<p>- short-term, episodic, semantic, and organizational memory;</p>
<p>- evaluation, observability, safety, incident, and kill-switch systems.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="autonomy-levels">Autonomy levels</h2>
<br />
<p>- A0: information only;</p>
<p>- A1: recommendation requiring human Decision;</p>
<p>- A2: automatic low-risk action with reviewable evidence;</p>
<p>- A3: bounded workflow autonomy under policy and approval thresholds;</p>
<p>- A4: exceptional high autonomy allowed only for explicitly certified domains.</p>
<br />
<p>Financial, legal, customs, quality release, custody, privileged security, and authoritative correction actions default to A0 or A1.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="agent-run-record">Agent run record</h2>
<br />
<p>Every run records tenant, principal, purpose, input, knowledge cutoff, context sources, prompt/model/tool versions, policy evaluations, approvals, tool calls, structured outputs, uncertainty, costs, observed outcomes, corrections, and evaluation.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="rag-and-memory">RAG and memory</h2>
<br />
<p>Retrieved text is evidence, not authority. Sources require access control, version, effective time, provenance, and freshness. Memory cannot silently create canonical business facts.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="evaluation">Evaluation</h2>
<br />
<p>Evaluate quality, factuality, safety, security, policy compliance, tool correctness, business outcome, latency, cost, drift, and subgroup performance. Promotions require sample thresholds, human approval, rollback, and incident controls.</p>
