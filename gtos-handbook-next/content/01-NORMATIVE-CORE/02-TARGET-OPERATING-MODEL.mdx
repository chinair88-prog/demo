<h2 id="product-organization">Product organization</h2>
<br />
<p>GTOS should be operated through durable product and platform teams aligned to bounded contexts, not temporary component projects.</p>
<br />
<h3 id="business-product-groups">Business product groups</h3>
<br />
<p>- Identity and Organization;</p>
<p>- Customer and CRM;</p>
<p>- Product, Catalog, Marketplace, Pricing, and Promotions;</p>
<p>- Procurement, Quotation, Contract, PI, and Purchase Order;</p>
<p>- Payment, Wallet, Billing, Finance Control, and Settlement;</p>
<p>- Manufacturing and Quality;</p>
<p>- Shipping, Customs, Provider, and Regulatory;</p>
<p>- Warehouse, Inventory, Fulfilment, Delivery, and Returns;</p>
<p>- Engagement and Communications.</p>
<br />
<h3 id="platform-groups">Platform groups</h3>
<br />
<p>- Developer Platform and API Gateway;</p>
<p>- Data Platform;</p>
<p>- Event and Workflow Platform;</p>
<p>- Identity and Security Platform;</p>
<p>- AI Platform and Model Governance;</p>
<p>- Observability and Reliability Platform;</p>
<p>- Cloud and Infrastructure Platform.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="decision-rights">Decision rights</h2>
<br />
<p>- Domain teams own business semantics and invariants.</p>
<p>- Platform teams own reusable technical capabilities and paved roads.</p>
<p>- Security and privacy authorities define mandatory controls and exceptions.</p>
<p>- Architecture governance resolves ownership and compatibility disputes.</p>
<p>- SRE defines production readiness and reliability policy.</p>
<p>- AI Governance approves autonomy, tools, evaluation, and kill-switch policy.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="delivery-model">Delivery model</h2>
<br />
<p>Work is delivered as vertical slices that include Domain behavior, persistence, contract, Portal journey, security, observability, tests, migration, runbook, and release evidence.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="governance-cadence">Governance cadence</h2>
<br />
<p>- weekly Domain design review;</p>
<p>- biweekly contract and integration review;</p>
<p>- monthly architecture and security council;</p>
<p>- quarterly resilience, disaster recovery, and AI governance review;</p>
<p>- release-specific production readiness review;</p>
<p>- annual Canon and Constitution review.</p>
