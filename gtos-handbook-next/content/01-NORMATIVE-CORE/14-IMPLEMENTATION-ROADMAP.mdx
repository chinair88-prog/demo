<br />
<h2 id="phase-0---governance-and-foundations">Phase 0 - Governance and foundations</h2>
<br />
<p>Adopt Canon, Constitution, Domain ownership, identity model, tenant model, engineering standards, ADR process, catalogues, CI quality gates, and production-readiness policy.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="phase-1---platform-paved-roads">Phase 1 - Platform paved roads</h2>
<br />
<p>Deliver identity, service templates, API gateway, secrets, observability, Event Bus, outbox/inbox, workflow engine, database migration framework, developer environment, test harnesses, and deployment platform.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="phase-2---core-commerce">Phase 2 - Core commerce</h2>
<br />
<p>Implement Product, Marketplace Offer, Price Version, Cart, Checkout Quote, Customer Order, Payment Intent, Inventory Reservation, and reliable Order saga.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="phase-3---trade-and-supply-chain">Phase 3 - Trade and supply chain</h2>
<br />
<p>Implement RFQ, Quotation, Contract, PI, PO, Manufacturing, Quality, documents, customs, shipping, custody, warehouse receiving, inventory, and fulfilment.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="phase-4---after-sales-and-finance">Phase 4 - After-sales and finance</h2>
<br />
<p>Implement delivery evidence, returns, claims, refund, settlement, reconciliation, reporting, and ESG evidence.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="phase-5---ai-native-operations">Phase 5 - AI-native operations</h2>
<br />
<p>Introduce retrieval, recommendations, planning, typed tools, supervised agents, evaluation, safety, and bounded autonomy after the underlying Domain controls are mature.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="phase-6---global-scale-and-certification">Phase 6 - Global scale and certification</h2>
<br />
<p>Add multi-region, jurisdiction profiles, advanced resilience, partner certification, data residency, high-assurance controls, cost optimization, and formal compliance evidence.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="prioritization-rule">Prioritization rule</h2>
<br />
<p>Build the smallest complete vertical slice, not isolated tables or screens. Platform foundations must be delivered just ahead of the slices that require them, avoiding both premature platform construction and repeated local reinvention.</p>
