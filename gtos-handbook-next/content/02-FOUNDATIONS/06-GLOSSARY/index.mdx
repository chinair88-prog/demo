<br />
<h2 id="aggregate">Aggregate</h2>
<br />
<p>A domain consistency boundary protecting business invariants.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="authoritative-truth">Authoritative Truth</h2>
<br />
<p>A governed Claim accepted within a declared authority boundary.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="authority">Authority</h2>
<br />
<p>The recognized right to make a binding assertion, decision, or action.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="canon">Canon</h2>
<br />
<p>A foundational GTOS axiom.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="claim">Claim</h2>
<br />
<p>A proposition asserted to be true.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="command">Command</h2>
<br />
<p>An authorized request to attempt an action.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="confidence">Confidence</h2>
<br />
<p>Qualified support for a Claim; not authority.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="correction">Correction</h2>
<br />
<p>A new assertion that qualifies or supersedes a prior assertion without erasing it.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="decision">Decision</h2>
<br />
<p>A governed commitment or determination.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="delegation">Delegation</h2>
<br />
<p>A bounded grant of authority.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="entity">Entity</h2>
<br />
<p>A distinguishable participant, object, place, instrument, document, or concept.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="event">Event</h2>
<br />
<p>An immutable assertion that a completed occurrence took place.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="evidence">Evidence</h2>
<br />
<p>Information used to support, challenge, or audit a Claim or Decision.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="execution">Execution</h2>
<br />
<p>An attempt to realize authorized Intent.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="history">History</h2>
<br />
<p>The preserved sequence of governed facts, observations, decisions, actions, and corrections.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="identity">Identity</h2>
<br />
<p>Continuity through which an Entity remains distinguishable across change.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="intent">Intent</h2>
<br />
<p>An authorized expression of desired future effect.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="knowledge-set">Knowledge Set</h2>
<br />
<p>Claims and evidence available to an observer or process at a time.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="mandate">Mandate</h2>
<br />
<p>Authority to pursue a class of Intent under constraints.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="observation">Observation</h2>
<br />
<p>An observer's assertion about reality.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="outcome">Outcome</h2>
<br />
<p>The observed result of Execution.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="perspective">Perspective</h2>
<br />
<p>A contextual projection or interpretation.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="policy">Policy</h2>
<br />
<p>A governed rule producing permission, obligation, prohibition, or required approval.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="provenance">Provenance</h2>
<br />
<p>Origin and transformation history.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="relationship">Relationship</h2>
<br />
<p>A business-significant association among independently identified Entities.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="state">State</h2>
<br />
<p>A scoped set of propositions about a subject at a time.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="truth-owner">Truth Owner</h2>
<br />
<p>The accountable authority governing a proposition.</p>
