<p>Within GTOS, <strong>Canon</strong> means the set of foundational axioms from which constitutional rules, reference architectures, engineering standards, and conformance tests are derived.</p>
<br />
<p>The Canon is intentionally small. Good ideas that are derivable, domain-specific, or technology-specific belong downstream.</p>
