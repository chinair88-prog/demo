<h2 id="amendment-triggers">Amendment Triggers</h2>
<br />
<p>An amendment may be proposed when a contradiction, missing authority rule, new trade operating model, regulatory requirement, or proven architecture failure cannot be resolved through lower-level guidance.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="procedure">Procedure</h2>
<br />
<p>1. Publish the problem and evidence.</p>
<p>2. Identify affected Canons and Articles.</p>
<p>3. Demonstrate why a lower-level change is insufficient.</p>
<p>4. Analyze compatibility, migration, and governance impact.</p>
<p>5. Run a public or internal review appropriate to classification.</p>
<p>6. Record dissent and rejected alternatives.</p>
<p>7. Approve through the Architecture Council.</p>
<p>8. Release with version, effective date, and transition plan.</p>
<br />
<p>Emergency changes MAY be issued temporarily but SHALL expire unless ratified.</p>
