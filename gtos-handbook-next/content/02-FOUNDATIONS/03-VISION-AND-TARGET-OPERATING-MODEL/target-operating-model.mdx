<br />
<p>GTOS operates as a federation of domain authorities connected through governed contracts and platform capabilities.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="participants">Participants</h2>
<br />
<p>- traders and manufacturers;</p>
<p>- logistics providers;</p>
<p>- ports and terminals;</p>
<p>- financial institutions and insurers;</p>
<p>- customs and regulatory authorities;</p>
<p>- inspection and certification bodies;</p>
<p>- platform operators;</p>
<p>- AI agents acting under mandates.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="coordination-pattern">Coordination Pattern</h2>
<br />
<p>Participants publish authoritative facts within scope, consume perspectives from others, form decisions under policy, express intent, execute through capabilities, and preserve outcomes as evidence.</p>
<br />
<p>GTOS does not require universal central ownership. It requires explicit ownership and trustworthy exchange.</p>
