<br />
<p>1. Model trade reality before software workflow.</p>
<p>2. Optimize for end-to-end evidence, not local data volume.</p>
<p>3. Automate decisions only after authority and knowledge boundaries are explicit.</p>
<p>4. Treat interoperability as semantic governance, not payload transport.</p>
<p>5. Build for jurisdictional variation without fragmenting core meaning.</p>
<p>6. Preserve human accountability while increasing machine capability.</p>
<p>7. Prefer reversible implementation choices and durable business semantics.</p>
<p>8. Design operations and recovery as part of architecture.</p>
