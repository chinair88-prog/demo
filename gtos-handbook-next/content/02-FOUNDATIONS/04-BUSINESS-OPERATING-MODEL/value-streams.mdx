<h2 id="trade-formation">Trade Formation</h2>
<br />
<p>Opportunity → qualification → offer → agreement → obligation.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="fulfilment">Fulfilment</h2>
<br />
<p>Order → allocation → shipment plan → booking → movement → delivery.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="border-compliance">Border Compliance</h2>
<br />
<p>Classification → screening → declaration → evidence → inspection or release.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="financial-settlement">Financial Settlement</h2>
<br />
<p>Obligation → invoice → payment decision → payment intent → settlement → reconciliation.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="exception-resolution">Exception Resolution</h2>
<br />
<p>Detection → qualification → assignment → evidence acquisition → decision → remediation → closure → learning.</p>
<br />
<p>Each value stream SHALL identify authoritative facts, decision points, required evidence, hand-offs, service expectations, and failure recovery.</p>
