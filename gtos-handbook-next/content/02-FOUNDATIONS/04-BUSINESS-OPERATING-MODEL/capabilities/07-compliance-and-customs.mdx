<h2 id="purpose">Purpose</h2>
<br />
<p>Evaluate controls, declarations, licensing, screening, inspections, release, and evidence.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="scope">Scope</h2>
<br />
<p>- screening</p>
<p>- classification checks</p>
<p>- declaration</p>
<p>- inspection</p>
<p>- release</p>

<h2 id="required-architecture">Required Architecture</h2>
<br />
<p>The capability SHALL define owner, authoritative propositions, actors, decisions, policies, state, commands, events, measures, service levels, data classifications, and Canon dependencies.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="key-measures">Key Measures</h2>
<br />
<p>- cycle time;</p>
<p>- first-time-right rate;</p>
<p>- exception rate;</p>
<p>- evidence completeness;</p>
<p>- decision reversal rate;</p>
<p>- automation coverage;</p>
<p>- service reliability.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="risks">Risks</h2>
<br />
<p>The principal failure is local optimization that breaks end-to-end truth, authority, or evidence. Capability reviews SHALL therefore include upstream and downstream value-stream impact.</p>
