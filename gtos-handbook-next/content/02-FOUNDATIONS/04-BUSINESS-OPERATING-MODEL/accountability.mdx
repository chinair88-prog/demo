<h2 id="raci-is-insufficient-alone">RACI Is Insufficient Alone</h2>
<br />
<p>GTOS additionally requires:</p>
<br />
<p>- truth owner;</p>
<p>- policy owner;</p>
<p>- decision authority;</p>
<p>- execution owner;</p>
<p>- evidence custodian;</p>
<p>- risk owner;</p>
<p>- service owner;</p>
<p>- data steward.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="decision-right-record">Decision-Right Record</h2>
<br />
<p>Every consequential decision class SHALL define:</p>
<br />
<p>- decision name and purpose;</p>
<p>- eligible authority;</p>
<p>- policy basis;</p>
<p>- minimum evidence;</p>
<p>- approval or escalation threshold;</p>
<p>- appeal and correction path;</p>
<p>- expected outcome measures.</p>
