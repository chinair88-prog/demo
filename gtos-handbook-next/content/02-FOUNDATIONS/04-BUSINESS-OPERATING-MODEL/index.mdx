<br />
<p>Business Architecture connects the Reference Theory to capabilities, value streams, actors, accountability, and measurable outcomes.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="rules">Rules</h2>
<br />
<p>- Capability ownership SHALL align with truth ownership.</p>
<p>- Value streams SHALL identify decisions and evidence dependencies.</p>
<p>- Business services SHALL publish outcomes and service expectations.</p>
<p>- Automation SHALL preserve decision authority and exception paths.</p>
