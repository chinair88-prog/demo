<h2 id="purpose">Purpose</h2>
<br />
<p>Epistemology governs what GTOS knows, why it believes it, who asserted it, when it became available, and how uncertainty or contradiction is preserved.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="observation">Observation</h2>
<br />
<p>An Observation is an observer's assertion about reality. Sensors, humans, organizations, external systems, documents, and AI models may produce observations.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="claim">Claim</h2>
<br />
<p>A Claim is a proposition asserted to be true. Claims may be authoritative, corroborated, disputed, inferred, or rejected.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="evidence">Evidence</h2>
<br />
<p>Evidence supports or challenges a Claim. Evidence has origin, integrity, scope, time, accessibility, and relevance.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="confidence">Confidence</h2>
<br />
<p>Confidence expresses support for a Claim; it is not truth. Confidence SHALL NOT be used to disguise the absence of authority.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="contradiction">Contradiction</h2>
<br />
<p>Contradiction is a first-class condition in which incompatible Claims coexist. GTOS preserves contradictions until an authorized resolution occurs.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="knowledge">Knowledge</h2>
<br />
<p>Knowledge is the set of Claims available to an observer or decision process at a time, together with provenance and qualification.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="decision">Decision</h2>
<br />
<p>A Decision is a governed commitment made using an Evidence Set, applicable policy, objectives, and authority.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="hindsight-discipline">Hindsight Discipline</h2>
<br />
<p>Decision review SHALL reconstruct the knowledge available at decision time. Later discoveries may affect learning, but SHALL NOT be presented as if they were previously available.</p>
