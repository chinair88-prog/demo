<br />
<h2 id="causality">Causality</h2>
<br />
<p>GTOS distinguishes temporal sequence from causality. An Event occurring before another Event does not prove causation.</p>
<br />
<p>Causal claims SHALL identify supporting evidence and assumptions.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="correlation-and-causation-identifiers">Correlation and Causation Identifiers</h2>
<br />
<p>- <code class="code-inline">correlationId</code> groups related work.</p>
<p>- <code class="code-inline">causationId</code> identifies the directly initiating fact or request.</p>
<p>- Neither field alone proves legal or scientific causality.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="correction">Correction</h2>
<br />
<p>A correction creates a new governed assertion that qualifies, supersedes, or invalidates a prior assertion. The original assertion remains historically visible.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="retraction">Retraction</h2>
<br />
<p>Retraction states that an assertion should no longer be relied upon. Retraction does not claim the assertion never existed.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="contradiction-resolution">Contradiction Resolution</h2>
<br />
<p>Resolution SHALL identify:</p>
<br />
<p>- contradictory claims;</p>
<p>- applicable authority;</p>
<p>- evidence considered;</p>
<p>- decision time;</p>
<p>- resulting authoritative claim;</p>
<p>- effect on downstream decisions;</p>
<p>- whether remediation is required.</p>
