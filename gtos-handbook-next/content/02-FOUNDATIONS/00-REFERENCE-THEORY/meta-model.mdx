<h2 id="dependency-chain">Dependency Chain</h2>
<br />
<CodeBlock code={"Reality\n ├─ contains → Entities\n │   ├─ persist through → Identity\n │   ├─ exist within → Time\n │   ├─ exhibit → State\n │   └─ participate in → Relationships\n ├─ changes through → Events\n └─ is perceived through → Observations\n      └─ support → Knowledge\n           └─ supports → Decisions\n                └─ authorizes → Intent\n                     └─ initiates → Execution\n                          └─ produces → Outcomes"} language="text" />
<br />
<p>History preserves Events, Observations, Decisions, Intent, Execution, Outcomes, and Corrections.</p>
<br />
<p>Authority constrains assertions, decisions, delegation, and execution.</p>
<br />
<p>Policy evaluates knowledge and context to produce permissions, obligations, or prohibitions.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="meta-model-invariants">Meta-Model Invariants</h2>
<br />
<p>1. No Relationship exists without independently identifiable participants.</p>
<p>2. No Event is meaningful without a subject, occurrence time, and transition semantics.</p>
<p>3. No historical Decision may be evaluated using information unavailable at decision time.</p>
<p>4. No Execution may claim legitimate business purpose without traceable Intent.</p>
<p>5. No derived view becomes authoritative merely through replication.</p>
<p>6. No correction erases the existence of the assertion being corrected.</p>
<p>7. No AI capability possesses authority unless authority has been explicitly delegated.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="canon-admission-test">Canon Admission Test</h2>
<br />
<p>A candidate Canon is admissible only when it passes:</p>
<br />
<p>- <strong>Independence:</strong> the concept is not reducible to another Canon.</p>
<p>- <strong>Necessity:</strong> removing it creates incoherence or makes a required GTOS property impossible.</p>
<p>- <strong>Derivation:</strong> lower-level standards can be logically derived from it.</p>
<p>- <strong>Placement:</strong> it has a unique location in the Meta-Model.</p>
<p>- <strong>Testability:</strong> architectural consequences can be inspected.</p>
