<h2 id="forms-of-uncertainty">Forms of Uncertainty</h2>
<br />
<p>GTOS recognizes:</p>
<br />
<p>- missing information;</p>
<p>- measurement error;</p>
<p>- ambiguous identity;</p>
<p>- model uncertainty;</p>
<p>- future uncertainty;</p>
<p>- policy ambiguity;</p>
<p>- disputed authority;</p>
<p>- contradictory evidence.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="rules">Rules</h2>
<br />
<p>- Unknown SHALL NOT be encoded as false.</p>
<p>- Estimated SHALL NOT be encoded as observed.</p>
<p>- Predicted SHALL NOT be encoded as decided.</p>
<p>- Confidence SHALL include method and calibration context where material.</p>
<p>- Decision thresholds SHALL be policy-controlled.</p>
<p>- High-impact uncertainty SHALL trigger escalation or evidence acquisition.</p>
<br />
<h2 id="ai-consequence">AI Consequence</h2>
<br />
<p>An AI agent must be able to say that the available evidence is insufficient. Fabricated certainty is a conformance failure.</p>
